Evidence gate

Success criteria determine when results enter the thesis.

09

A result becomes chapter-ready only when the implementation, evaluation, and experimental record tell the same story. Passing one metric is not sufficient.

01 · INTEGRITY

Aggregation tests pass

Model keys, tensor shapes, dtypes, sample weights, checkpoint loading, and non-trainable buffers are verified explicitly.

02 · EQUIVALENCE

One-client FL matches local training

From the same initialization and configuration, a one-client federation round must agree with direct local training.

03 · DISCRIMINATION

Two-client behavior is non-degenerate

Specificity, confusion matrices, predicted probabilities, and AUROC confirm that the model is not defaulting to one class.

04 · COMPARISON

FedAvg and FedProx are compared fairly

Both algorithms use identical partitions, seeds, model initialization, metrics, and reporting of variation across sites.

Decision rule: only after all four gates pass may the thesis report a federated performance claim or interpret a FedProx effect. Until then, values remain provisional debugging evidence.

Required audit trail: configuration manifest, dataset split identifiers, source commit, per-round metrics, evaluation outputs, and saved checkpoints.
