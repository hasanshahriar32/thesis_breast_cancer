Interpretation standard

FedProx is a hypothesis, not a guarantee.

08

Baseline · FedAvg

Iterative model averaging

Clients train locally on their own data, then the server forms a shared model by aggregating local updates, commonly weighted by sample count.

Purpose: establish the federated baseline.
Risk: non-IID clients can drift in different directions between rounds.

Tested extension · FedProx

Local training with a proximal constraint

FedProx adds a penalty that discourages a client model from moving too far from the received global model during local optimization.

Purpose: improve stability under statistical and systems heterogeneity.
Requirement: verify the proximal term, μ value, and comparison protocol in this implementation.

Thesis reporting rule: state the chosen μ, partitions, local-epoch schedule, and per-round outcomes. Report FedProx as beneficial only if it improves the pre-specified metrics under the same reproducible protocol as FedAvg.

Sources: McMahan et al. (2017), Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep Networks from Decentralized Data; Li et al. (2020), Federated Optimization in Heterogeneous Networks.
